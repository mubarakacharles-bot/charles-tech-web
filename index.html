🏢 About Charles Tech

Charles Tech is the trading name of CHARLES HIGH TECHNOLOGY SOLUTION Ltd (CHTS), a Rwandan company specializing in Embedded Systems and IoT Smart project solutions. We're based in Musanze District, Rwanda.

We help students, hobbyists, and organizations across Rwanda build real embedded electronics and IoT projects — supplying the components, kits, tools, and step-by-step documentation needed to go from idea to working prototype. Our directory of Embedded Systems and IoT projects is built for learners at every level, from a first LED-blink circuit to full smart-monitoring systems.

MANAGED FROM OUR OWN WORKSHOP

Behind every kit and project on this site is our workshop in Musanze — where our manager and team design in SolidWorks, prep prints in PrusaSlicer, wire up embedded systems and sensor networks, and run everything through 3D printing, PCB design, and automation before it ever reaches a customer. It's hands-on work, done by people who build what they sell.

What we do

  • Source and sell Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ESP32, sensors, and related components
  • Design and document embedded systems and IoT project kits with full tools and materials lists
  • Provide guidance and adviser support for students and makers planning their own projects
  • Build custom embedded/IoT solutions for organizations and individuals on request

🎓 Courses & Training We Offer

Beyond components and project kits, Charles Tech runs hands-on training that takes a learner from a 3D design on screen to a finished, working electronic enclosure and a professionally printed circuit board.

🖥️
3D Design (CAD)
Software: SolidWork

Learn to model parts and enclosures from scratch in SolidWork — sketches, extrusions, assemblies, and preparing a model for 3D printing.

🍕
Slicing & Print Prep
Software: PrusaSlicer

Convert your 3D model into printable G-code — supports, infill, layer height, and settings tuned for enclosure printing.

🖨️
3D Printing of Electronic Enclosures
Software: Pronterface

Control and monitor your 3D printer directly, and print a complete, fitted enclosure for your electronic/IoT project.

🧩
PCB Design
Software: KiCad & Proteus Professional

Design schematics and PCB layouts in KiCad, then simulate and verify circuit behaviour in Proteus Professional before production.

⚙️
PCB Printing on CNC (Finishing)
Software: Universal G-Code Sender (UGCS)

Take your finished PCB design and mill/print it on a CNC machine using Universal G-Code Sender, completing the journey from design to a physical board.

Courses can be taken individually or as a full pathway — 3D Design → Slicing → 3D-Printed Enclosure → PCB Design → CNC PCB Printing. Contact us to ask about schedules, group/school pricing, and equipment access.
